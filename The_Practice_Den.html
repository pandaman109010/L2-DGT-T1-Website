Start with the Basics: The 3-Club Cascade

Before you can master complex patterns, you must start with the foundation of all club juggling: the 3-Club Cascade. This is the first pattern you should learn, following the same three-step approach used for rings, scarves, or balls. Remember, in this journey, dropping is a sign of progress!

Step 1: The Single Flip

Begin by practicing a single club throw from side to side. Aim for an arc slightly above head height. Focus on throwing upwards rather than away from your body. The club should complete one full spin, allowing you to catch it cleanly by the handle end.

Step 2: The Exchange

Hold one club in each hand. Toss the first club as you did in Step 1. As it reaches its peak, toss the second club inside the path of the first one to the same height.

Step 3: The Full Cascade

Hold two clubs in your dominant hand and one in your other hand. Launch the first club from the hand holding two. When it peaks, toss the club from the opposite hand. As that one peaks, throw the third club, and continue the rhythm. Experiment with your grip to find a comfortable way to hold two clubs at once.

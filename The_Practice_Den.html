Common Pitfalls & Quick Fixes

Over spinning

The issue:The club spins too fast could and hit you in the face.

The fix:You are likely holding the club too high up the neck. Move your hand down toward the knob (the end of the handle) to get a slower, more controlled flip.

The "Forward March"

The issue:You find yourself walking across the room to catch the clubs.

The fix:Imagine a pane of glass in front of your face. You must throw the clubs parallel to that glass, not through it. Practice standing with your toes against a wall to force a vertical throw.

Chicken Wings (Elbows out)

The issue:Your elbows are flaring out, making your catches jerky.

The fix:Keep your elbows tucked in at your hips. Most of the power should come from a small "scooping" motion of your forearms and a flick of the wrist.

Section 3: Milestone Checklist

Don't rush to the next level. Use this checklist to ensure your fundamentals are 'Excellence' standard before moving on.

1.   The 10-Throw Streak:10 perfect single flips with your non-dominant hand.

2.  The Quiet Catch:Catching the club by the handle without a loud 'slap' sound.

3.   The Statue:Juggling 3 clubs for 5 seconds without moving your feet.

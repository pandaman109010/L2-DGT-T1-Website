Privacy Policy for Juggler’s Toolkit

Last Updated: 19 March 2026

At Juggler’s Toolkit (https://club-juggling.web.app), accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Juggler’s Toolkit and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect any personal information from you, such as your name, email address, or phone number. We do not provide a contact form or account registration that stores personal data on our servers.

2. Automated Data Collection & Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or track your behavior across websites:

  • Google AdSense: We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Google uses cookies (such as the DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings.
  • Google Firebase: We use Firebase to host and manage website functionality. Firebase may collect certain diagnostic or usage data to help us understand how the site is performing.
  • Embedded Content: This site may include embedded YouTube videos. These third-party websites may collect data about you, use cookies, or embed additional third-party tracking.

3. Log Files

Like most websites, Juggler’s Toolkit follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, and referring/exit pages. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

4. How We Use Your Information

The information collected by third-party services is used to:

  • Personalize the advertisements you see.
  • Analyze website traffic and performance.
  • Maintain the technical stability of the site via Firebase.

5. Your Rights & Data Control

Since we do not store your personal data directly, we do not have "user accounts" to delete. However, you have rights regarding the data collected by our third-party partners:

  • Cookies: You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options.
  • Ad Personalization: You can manage how Google uses your data for ads via the Google My Ad Center.
  • New Zealand Privacy Act: In accordance with New Zealand law, you have the right to ask for a copy of any personal information we (or our processors) hold about you, and to ask for it to be corrected if you think it is wrong.

6. Security & Storage

We do not maintain an internal database of user information. All technical data is handled by our service providers (Google Firebase and AdSense) using industry-standard security protocols.

Start with the Basics: The 3-Club Cascade

Before you learn and master complex patterns, you need to start with the foundation of all of club juggling: the 3-Club Cascade. This is the first pattern you should learn when starting out. It follows the same patterns with rings and balls. Remember, this is a journey, so have fun with it.

Step 1: The Single Flip

Begin by practising throwing a single club from each hand back and forth. aim for an arc slightly above head height. Focus on throwing up and across rather than forward or away from your body. The club should complete one exactly full spin, not too fast, not too slow. This allows you to catch the handle cleanly in the other hand.

Step 2: The Exchange

hold one club in each hand, toss the first club as you did in step 1, and when it reaches its peak, toss the second club with the same path, don't worry, they shouldn't hit each other, and try not to rush it.

Step 3: The Full Cascade

hold two clubs in your dominant hand. (There are 2 different grips called the normal grip and the Circus Grip. Google what they look like and try them out; they should fit comfortably. I personally prefer the Circus Grip, but pick whatever feels right for you, then launch the first club from the hand holding two. Just before it peaks, throw the second club in the opposite hand. You will need to catch the first club in that hand, and when the second club peaks, chuck the 3rd club. Have a look at the practice den for proper tutorials

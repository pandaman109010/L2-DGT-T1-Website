Common mistakes & Quick Fixes

Over spinning

The issue:The club starts spinning too fast, and it's hard to catch.

The fix:You are likely rushing or holding the club too high to the neck. move your hand closer to the knob (the end of the handle) and try to slow down to get a more controlled flip.

The "Forward March"

The issue:You find yourself walking across the park to catch the clubs.

The fix:Imagine a pane of glass in front of your face. You can throw the clubs through the glass, but you can't move past it. You can also try throwing the clubs more with your wrists twisting sideways, then forward and clench your core. Remember not to rush.

Chicken Wings (Elbows out)

The issue:You're flaring your elbows out, making your catches jerky and look funny.

The fix:Try to relax more; it should flow with your body. If you find yourself tensing up, you're probably throwing too fast or too slow. You can also try tucking your elbows at your hips, remember most of the power should come from the small “scooping” motion of your forearms and wrists.

Section 3: Milestone Checklist

Try not to rush to the next trick. make sure you have the basics nailed before you move on to something more complicated. And don't pick anything too hard for your next trick.

1.   The 10-Throw Streak:10 perfect single flips with your non-dominant hand. this proves that you can keep the patten going

2.  The Quiet Catch:Catching the club by the handle without a loud 'slap' sound. this shows that you have the correct spin and control.

3.   The Statue:Juggling 3 clubs for 5 seconds without moving your feet. this shows that you have control over the angle of your clubs.

Privacy Policy for Juggler’s Toolkit

Updated on March 26, 2026

One of our top concerns at Juggler's Toolkit (https://club-juggling.web.app) is visitor privacy. The kinds of data that Juggler's Toolkit gathers, records, and uses are described in this Privacy Policy document.

1. Data That We Gather

No personal information, including your name, phone number, or email address, is directly collected from you. This website does not contain any registration forms or account creation procedures that store personal information on our internal systems.

2. Third-party services and automated data collection

We make use of third-party services that might gather data that could be used to identify you or monitor your online activity. These services consist of:

  • Google AdSense: We serve ads using Google AdSense. Google employs cookies, like the DART cookie, to display advertisements in response to your visits to this and other websites. By going to Google Ads Settings, you can choose not to receive personalized ads.
  • Google Firebase: We use Firebase for website infrastructure and hosting. To help us understand how the site is operating and to guarantee technical stability, Firebase may gather specific usage or diagnostic data.
  • Embedded Content: YouTube videos may be embedded on this website. These third-party websites might use cookies, gather personal information about you, or incorporate other third-party tracking.

3. Log Files

Juggler’s Toolkit follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, and referring/exit pages. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

4. How We Use Your Information

The information collected by third-party services is used to:

  • Deliver and personalize the advertisements you see.
  • Analyze website traffic and technical performance.
  • Maintain the technical stability and security of the site.

5. Your Rights & Data Control

Since we do not store your personal data directly, we do not have "user accounts" to manage or delete. However, you have rights regarding the data collected by our third-party partners:

  • Cookies: You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options.
  • Ad Personalization: You can manage how Google uses your data for ads via the Google My Ad Center.
  • New Zealand Privacy Act: In accordance with New Zealand law, you have the right to ask for a copy of any personal information held about you and to ask for it to be corrected if it is wrong.

6. Security & Storage

Juggler’s Toolkit does not host its own storage for user data. All data processing and storage are handled by our service providers (Google Firebase and AdSense) using industry-standard security protocols.
